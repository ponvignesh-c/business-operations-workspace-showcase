# Demo Walkthrough

This is the short scenario used to demonstrate the product without real business data.

1. Start from **Overview** and follow the first-time setup cards.
2. Under **Inventory**, create a fictional material such as “Demo hardwood plank.”
3. Under **Purchases**, add a fictional supplier and save a draft for 20 units.
4. Confirm the purchase after the hypothetical goods arrive; stock now shows 20 units.
5. Under **Sales**, add a fictional customer and create a draft invoice for 5 units.
6. Confirm dispatch; stock now shows 15 units.
7. Under **Operations**, create a delivery record and mark it delivered.
8. Under **Reports**, review the summary and low-stock indicator.
9. As an administrator, review the audit entries produced by the confirmation actions.

The point of the demo is the relationship between physical business events and their system effect, not data entry for its own sake.
